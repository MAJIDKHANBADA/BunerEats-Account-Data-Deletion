BunerEats – Account & Data Deletion
App Name: BunerEats (com.buner.eats.bada)
Developer: Bada
Support Email: majidkhanbada@gmail.com
You can permanently delete your BunerEats account and associated personal data at any time using either method below.
1. Delete Your Account Directly Inside the App (Instant)
Open the BunerEats app and sign in to your account.
For Customers: Tap the Account tab in the bottom navigation bar, then tap Delete All Data.
For Store Owners & Delivery Riders: Tap the ⋮ Settings menu at the top right, then tap Delete All Data.
Confirm the first prompt, then type DELETE to complete verification.
Your profile information, saved delivery addresses, local cache, and active authentication session are cleared immediately.
2. Request Account Deletion Without Reinstalling the App
If you have uninstalled BunerEats or cannot access your device, please email us from your registered Google email address:
Email To: majidkhanbada@gmail.com
Subject: BunerEats Account & Data Deletion Request
Include: Your registered Google email address and registered phone number (so we can verify account ownership).
Verified email deletion requests are processed within 7 business days, and we will reply once deletion is complete.
Data Permanently Deleted
Google Sign-In / Firebase Authentication link and session tokens
Profile information (full name, email address, phone number, alternate delivery phone)
Saved delivery addresses, landmarks, and map pinpoint coordinates
Local app cache, cart items, and preferences
Uploaded pharmacy prescription photos once any active order is completed
Data Temporarily Retained
Completed order invoices and wallet/settlement transaction records may be retained for up to 90 days (or longer where required by tax, anti-fraud, or dispute-resolution rules) in restricted audit form, after which personal identifiers are purged.
